Mines FSAE

Digital Board • Technical Design Writeup

Project Overview

The Digital Board serves as the central data acquisition (DAQ) and processing brain for Mines Formula SAE. It captures critical telemetry from across the vehicle, handles sensor aggregation, high-speed Ethernet communications, and precise positioning.

Hardware Architecture & Key Design Highlights

High-Speed Processing: Powered by an STM32H5 series ARM Cortex-M33 microcontroller running at high clock rates, featuring integrated crypto accelerators and dedicated DMA channels for uninterrupted data throughput.

100-BaseTX Ethernet & Discrete Magnetics: Implemented discrete magnetic termination circuits to interface the Ethernet PHY over RMII and MDI. Routed differential pairs with matched 100Ω differential impedance, continuous reference planes, and length-matching within strict 5 mil tolerances.

Dual-Band GNSS: Integrated a high-precision L1/L5 dual-frequency GNSS receiver for sub-meter velocity and trajectory logging with dedicated RF trace geometry (50Ω coplanar waveguide with ground) and low-noise LDO power filtering.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Microcontroller STM32H563 ARM Cortex-M33
Layer Stackup 4-Layer FR-4 (Signal - GND - PWR - Signal)
Ethernet Interface 100-BaseTX (RMII / MDI Discrete Termination)
Positioning Module Dual-Band L1/L5 Concurrent GNSS
Controlled Impedance 100Ω Diff (Ethernet) / 50Ω SE (RF)
CAD Software Altium Designer

Testing, Validation & Bring-Up

Verified signal integrity across RMII clock lines and differential TX/RX pairs with high-bandwidth oscilloscopes. Measured return loss and eye diagrams to validate reliable Ethernet link negotiation under noisy automotive motor-controller environments.

BSPD • Technical Design Writeup

Safety System Overview

The Brake System Plausibility Device (BSPD) is a critical safety circuit mandated by Formula SAE competition rules (EV 4.7 / IC rules). Its role is to continuously monitor both brake pedal force and throttle position to immediately and irreversibly trip the vehicle's shutdown circuit if hard braking occurs simultaneously with substantial power delivery.

Non-Programmable Discrete Architecture

Per Formula SAE rules, the BSPD must not contain programmable logic, microcontrollers, or software. The entire logic is implemented through discrete analog and digital ICs:

Analog Window Comparators: Precision comparator networks monitor the analog voltage outputs from hydraulic pressure transducers and throttle position sensors (TPS), with low-pass RC filters to eliminate mechanical vibration noise.

Time-Delay & Counting Logic: Built with hardware decade and binary counter ICs clocked by an onboard precision oscillator to establish an exact, immutable 500ms trip threshold before asserting an emergency shutdown latch.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Circuit Type 100% Non-Programmable Hardware Logic
Trip Condition Hard Brake (>30 bar) + Active Power (>5kW) > 500ms
Logic Components Analog Comparators, Binary Counters, Decade Decoders
Latching Topology Hardware Set/Reset Latch (Power-Cycle Reset Only)
Simulation Tool LTSpice (Monte Carlo & Worst-Case Tolerance Analysis)
CAD Tool Altium Designer

Simulation & Rule Compliance

Simulated extensively in LTSpice to verify timing stability across temperature swings (-40°C to +85°C) and supply rail fluctuations, guaranteeing false-trip immunity while guaranteeing 100% compliance during technical inspection.

BSPD Relay • Technical Design Writeup

Power Switching Architecture

The BSPD Relay Board is an auxiliary high-reliability power management PCB designed to interface directly with the main BSPD. It provides rapid, galvanic disconnect of vehicle shutdown loops carrying continuous loads up to 7 Amperes.

Bi-Directional MOSFET Solid State Design

Unlike traditional mechanical relays that suffer from contact bounce, contact welding, and shock-induced vibration disconnects during competition racing, this custom design utilizes an ultra-low $R_{DS(on)}$ bi-directional N-channel power MOSFET solid state relay (SSR) stage.

High-Current Copper Geometry: Sized high-copper polygon pours on 2oz outer layers with thermal via arrays to dissipate heat efficiently under continuous 7A conduction without requiring bulky heatsinks.

Galvanic Isolation: Optocoupled gate drivers provide complete isolation between noisy shutdown actuator coils and sensitive low-voltage logic circuitry.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Specification
Continuous Current Rating 7.0 Amperes Continuous
Switching Device Back-to-Back Dual Power MOSFETs (SSR)
On-Resistance ($R_{DS(on)}$) < 15 mΩ Total Conduction Path
Copper Weight 2 oz Cu with Thermal Via Stitching
Isolation Voltage 2.5 kV Galvanic Isolation
Actuation Response Time < 150 μs (Fast Shutdown Response)

Thermal & Load Validation

Thermally profiled under full 7A continuous current test cycles with an infrared thermal camera. Measured peak junction temperature rise of less than 18°C above ambient, proving robust thermal headroom.
